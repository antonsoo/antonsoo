## About

- **Founder & CEO of [PRAVIEL](https://praviel.com)**: ancient languages, spoken aloud again. Live on iOS, Android, and the web.
- **AI/ML and software engineer.** Master's in AI from Tokyo Tech (speech recognition), production ML at Quantiphi, then LLM agents and safety research. I take contract work too.
- **Open source.** Small tools for people who build with LLMs, and fixes merged into ESPnet, Kornia, Pipecat, raylib, and others.
- **AGI** has had my attention for over a decade. As a kid I meant to be a physics professor, until I figured physics would get further with AI. Still into physics, math, philosophy, history, and music.

## Upstream fixes

Merged into other people's projects by their maintainers. Three favorites:

- **ESPnet** ([#6865](https://github.com/espnet/espnet/pull/6865)): a training run that had stopped early trained one more epoch every time it was resumed. Stopped now means stopped.
- **Pipecat** ([#5989](https://github.com/pipecat-ai/pipecat/pull/5989)): Deepgram's HTTP voice could end a chunk halfway through a 16-bit sample, and a voice agent could go mute for the rest of the call.
- **raylib** ([#6198](https://github.com/raysan5/raylib/pull/6198), [#6199](https://github.com/raysan5/raylib/pull/6199)): BDF fonts load without crashing, and a malformed OBJ model no longer sends the loader reading past its own vertices.
