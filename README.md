By training an AI researcher; by calling, a teacher of very old languages. At the moment that means teaching the world to read Latin, Greek, and Hebrew out loud, and building the tools that make it stick.

<img src="assets/divider-gold.svg" alt="" width="100%" />

## Dē mē &nbsp;·&nbsp; about

- Founder, CEO, and President of **[PRAVIEL](https://praviel.com)**, a lessons-first app for learning ancient languages as living, speakable systems. Latin, Koine and Classical Greek, Biblical Hebrew, and more on the way.
- Graduate training in **artificial intelligence**. Before PRAVIEL there were several startups, a tall stack of ML projects, and a stubborn belief that old texts deserve modern tools.
- I care about pronunciation, provenance, and shipping. Order depends on the day.
- My bio reads **ΓΝΩΘΙ ΣΑΥΤΟΝ**, *know thyself*. Still a work in progress.
