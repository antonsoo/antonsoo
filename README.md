By training an AI researcher; by calling, a teacher of very old languages. That means teaching the world to read and speak eleven of them out loud, from Latin and Ancient Greek to Gothic and Classical Chinese, and building the tools that make it stick.

<img src="assets/divider-gold.svg" alt="" width="100%" />

## Dē mē &nbsp;·&nbsp; about

- Founder & CEO of **[PRAVIEL](https://praviel.com)**, a lessons-first app for learning ancient languages as living, speakable systems. Eleven of them so far, from Latin and Greek to Sanskrit and Classical Chinese, with more in the works.
- A master's in **artificial intelligence** from the Institute of Science Tokyo. Before PRAVIEL: speech recognition on supercomputers, LLM agents, a startup or two, and a stubborn belief that old texts deserve modern tools.
- I care about pronunciation, provenance, and shipping. Order depends on the day.
- My bio reads **ΓΝΩΘΙ ΣΑΥΤΟΝ**, *know thyself*. Still a work in progress.

## Linguae &nbsp;·&nbsp; the tongues

<div align="center">

<img src="assets/linguae.svg" alt="The languages PRAVIEL teaches, each in its native script: Classical Chinese, Classical Arabic, Classical Sanskrit, Classical Latin, Ancient Greek, Hebrew, Aramaic, Church Slavonic, Middle English, and Gothic. Ancient Egyptian, in hieroglyphs, is also taught in the app." width="100%" />

<sub>Each language PRAVIEL teaches, set in its own script and baked to vector paths: the Arabic joins, the Sanskrit ligates, the Hebrew and Aramaic run right to left, and all of it looks right in any browser. Egyptian hieroglyphs are taught in the app too.</sub>
